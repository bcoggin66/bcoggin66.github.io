Grayton Beach State Park

For my Eagle Scout service project, I led the installation of a roughly 200-foot water connection running from the back of a cabin to a fire pit area at Grayton Beach State Park. The project also included a wood storage shed, a spigot, and a hose to make water and firewood more accessible at the site.

I coordinated the work and helped bring the different parts of the project together. It was a hands-on experience in planning, teamwork, and problem-solving, and it gave me an opportunity to contribute something useful to the park.
